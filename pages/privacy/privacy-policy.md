Please refer to the [NIST Internal Report 8588 "A Community-Driven Differential Privacy Registry"](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ir/8588/ipd) for all proposed governance and policies for this project.  **The public comment period is open through November 14, 2025. NIST invites the public to comment on the plan and especially welcomes contributions that will help maximize the value of the DP Registry.**
